The Challenge

Complex Products, Wait Times & AI Risks

Information Asymmetry

Over 68% of retail clients struggle to comprehend tiered deposit rates, notice intervals, and government subsidies (e.g. FLISP).

Support Bottlenecks

Branch desks and call queues are overwhelmed answering repetitive tier-1 product rules rather than relationship building.

Generic AI Hallucinations & Liability

Public LLMs hallucinate non-existent banking terms, fail to comply with the National Credit Act (NCA), and breach South Africa's POPIA privacy standards.

The Solution

Nedbank SmartFin Full-Stack Portal

NedPulse Advisory Copilot

Multi-turn conversational banking copilot guiding customers through accounts, savings tiers, and loan products.

Vector-Grounded RAG Engine

Indexes 2,500+ words of official Nedbank product documentation, producing verified answers with cited source chunk snippets.

3-Sentence Contract Summarizer

Distills lengthy financial disclosure terms into 3 executive sentences with transparent token cost tracking.

Audited Advisory Feed

Full-featured post and announcement publishing platform with role-based author controls.

AI Engineering

Grounded RAG Pipeline & Zero-Hallucination Guardrails

Semantic Chunking Architecture

Documents partitioned into 500-character windows with 50-character sliding overlaps to protect semantic boundaries.

Cosine Vector Search

Dense embedding vector comparisons execute in sub-150ms latency with strict relevance thresholds.

POPIA & Compliance Verification

Strict prompt boundaries enforce that any query outside the verified Nedbank knowledge base is rejected with an explicit disclosure.

Backend & API (Render)
